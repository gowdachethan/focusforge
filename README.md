# FOCUSFORGE – GATE 2027 + Robotics & AI

Personal Study Operating System built with **React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, IndexedDB, Recharts, Lucide Icons, and Offline PWA Support**.

- **Live App (GitHub Pages):** https://gowdachethan.github.io/focusforge/
- **Exam Target:** GATE 2027 Robotics and Automation (RA) + 15 Robotics & AI Technical Skills

## Features

- **Dashboard & Timetable (`Asia/Kolkata`)**: Pre-loaded Monday–Saturday & Sunday schedules, full slot editing, missed-task gentle catch-up, and live GATE 2027 countdown.
- **Smart Study Launcher & Clock-Anchored Timer**: Focus screen with Pomodoro, Standard, Deep Work, and Custom modes anchored to `targetEndTimestamp` so background tabs stay accurate without duplicate session logs.
- **Official GATE 2027 RA Syllabus**: General Aptitude (GA), Engineering Mathematics (A.1), Basics of Mechatronics (A.2), Principles of Robotics & Automation (A.3), Control Systems (B1.3), Mechanical Engineering – Part B2 (B2.1–B2.3), and Part B1.1–B1.2.
- **15 Robotics & AI Skills**: ROS 2, Python, C++, Computer Vision, OpenCV, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Gazebo, SLAM, Navigation2, Reinforcement Learning, NVIDIA Isaac Sim, Edge AI, Industrial Automation, and Embedded Robotics.
- **Topic-Wise Tests & Fullscreen Mock Exams**: MCQ, MSQ, and NAT scoring per official GATE rules (`-1/3`, `-2/3`, `0` for MSQ/NAT), virtual scientific calculator, and weak-topic diagnostics.
- **Spaced Repetition, Notes & Backup**: Day 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 revision queue, searchable notes with image/file attachments, Recharts analytics from real completed records, and JSON/CSV backup & restore.
